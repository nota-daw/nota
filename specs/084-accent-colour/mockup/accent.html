Choose the accent colour — specs/084 · nota-daw/nota#311

Every colour on this page is computed from the theme files by the derivation in research.md: Orange is the theme files verbatim; another accent keeps each accent token's lightness relation to the theme's own orange and swaps hue and saturation, then — only if the readability bar would fail — moves the lightness by the smallest step that passes. Click a swatch or a theme to try it. The swatches, the Custom button and the picker are the new controls; everything else is the existing UI wearing the accent.

Accent sits directly under the theme picker. Seven presets, then Custom; the chosen one carries a 2 px ring in TextPrimary (colour only — the ring slot is always there, so nothing moves). A swatch's name is its tooltip; the chosen name is the readout at the end of the row. The theme cards preview each theme with the chosen accent.

Presets: Orange (default) · Lime · Aqua · Sky · Violet · Magenta · Pink — no yellow (mute), red (record), green (meter) or mid blue (solo). Each passes T3.4, T3.5, T3.8, the meter green and every track colour (ΔE ≥ 15) in all four themes — the smoke test enforces it.

2 · Custom — the picker (a popover under the Custom button)

Saturation/brightness area, hue strip, the old/new chip and a hex field (Geist Mono; typing a valid value applies at once). The In themes strip shows the colour as each theme will wear it; a dot marks a theme where its lightness was moved to stay readable. Everything applies live while dragging; clicking outside keeps it and saves; Esc puts back the accent worn before the picker opened. A colour no lightness can rescue (a grey, or one that sits on the record red / mute / solo / meter green in some theme) is not applied: the note says why in Danger ink and the accent stays as it was. The spectrum is the one gradient in the app — content, like a waveform — listed in DESIGN.md's accepted exceptions.
