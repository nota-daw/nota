States to check

  • Editing B — the B key is the only accent in the morph column; the morph strip’s “B” end takes the accent too. Every knob shows the B value; A’s value is the faint tick outside the groove.
  • Morph between (35 % demo) — each knob shows a muted dot on the groove where the sound is now.
  • Effect off — the section’s body takes Inactive: inner switches that are on turn Rule, arcs Rule, labels Ink 5. Nothing orange inside an effect that is off.
  • Detune is inactive unless the voice mode is Unison; Division replaces Rate while LFO / delay Sync is on.
  • Layer / Split — Lower | Upper appears in the tab row; Voice’s Output section becomes Part (Volume, Pan, Octave, Split key with Learn, voice share).
  • Both Studio themes (toggle above); Ember themes follow from tokens.
