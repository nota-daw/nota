The biggest Nota release yet: a new look you can make your own, recording on many tracks at once, device cards rebuilt around their graphs, a browser for your whole computer and keyboard shortcuts you choose yourself.

**1. A new look, and it's yours**
The shell is flat and grey, so the track colours carry the music, and orange means one thing at a time: solid for what is live, a soft tint for what you picked. Prefer how 0.59 looked? Settings → Appearance → Look → Classic brings it back, with any colour theme. Or drop your own theme file into the Themes folder: colours, a typeface and corner roundness. Text size, phrase lines every 4–32 bars and group trays in the Arrangement come along.

**2. Record the whole band in one pass**
Arm several audio tracks and Record takes them all at once, each from its own input of the interface, sample-aligned. Every armed instrument track records too, each from its own MIDI device and channel, and several Session slots can record in one take. On an interface with more outputs, a private Cue mix goes to your headphones while Main goes to the audience.

**3. Device cards built around their graphs**
Every built-in effect lays out like Auto Filter: the graph fills its tab with its knobs beside it, and the right panel follows the tab. Most effects fold to a small S card. The instruments share one filter graph with the live spectrum and one envelope graph with curves and Lin / Exp. EQ-8, Dynamic EQ-8, Lens and Auto Filter open XL over the chain or in their own window, and Reverb and Delay draw what your audio actually does.

**4. A browser for your whole computer**
Add your own sample folders, all indexed and searched. Browse any folder or drive from the Files tab, and Locate a clip's file in the browser or in Finder / Explorer. The Projects tab lists your newest work first, with tempo, key and length.

**5. Your own keyboard shortcuts**
Settings → Shortcuts: every key in Nota can be changed, including the computer-keyboard notes. Conflicts are caught before saving, changed keys are marked, and each one resets on its own.

**6. Articulation maps**
Import a Cubase expression map or a Studio One keyswitch list, pick the technique per note in the piano roll, and Nota sends the keyswitches to your sample library just ahead of the notes.

**7. Also new**
Custom wavetables in Nota Aurora, a Modular tab in the browser with the Arrangement's track headers beside the canvas, a ☰ Menu key in place of the menu bar on Windows and Linux, a ⋯ menu that keeps the whole transport bar in reach, and Nota Remote in the new look with clip previews on its Scenes screen.
