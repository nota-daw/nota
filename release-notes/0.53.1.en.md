Nota 0.53 is about the mix: a Stereo Map that shows where every sound sits, panning that keeps stereo tracks whole, and undo for every fader and pan move. The piano roll gets faster note editing, and plug-ins are more reliable on every system.

**1. Stereo Map**
The Mixer has a second view: switch to **Stereo Map** in its title bar. Every track, return and the master is a dot at its pan and volume, with a cloud around it showing where the sound really is, how wide and how loud: a mono sound is a narrow cloud on its dot, a wide pad spreads across the map. The master's phase correlation is shown too. Drag a dot to set pan and volume at once, double-click to centre it, ⌥ double-click for 0 dB, hover for the numbers, click to select the track.

**2. Panning that keeps stereo whole**
Panning a stereo track or return now moves its whole image instead of dropping one channel: at full left, both channels play from the left speaker. Mono material and centred tracks sound exactly as before. New projects use the new **Stereo** pan law. Existing projects keep **Balance** and sound the same; the Mixer marks them **Older project**, and the **PAN LAW** switch in its footer changes the law at any time, even during playback, without a click. Group strips stay on Balance (marked **BAL**), so grouping doesn't change levels.

**3. Undo for volume and pan**
Fader, pan and master volume moves can now be undone and redone with **⌘Z / ⌘⇧Z**, wherever you made them: the Mixer, track headers, the Return and Master rows, the Session mixer, the transport's **MST**, Nota Remote, a MIDI controller or the AI assistant. One move is one step in the same history as every other edit.

**4. Faster note editing in the piano roll**
**⇧← / ⇧→** shorten or lengthen the selected notes by one grid step, **⌥↑ / ⌥↓** change their velocity by 10 (**⌥⇧↑ / ⌥⇧↓** by 1). A note moved, stretched, drawn or pasted onto a note of the same pitch now replaces it instead of stacking on top: the note underneath is cut or removed when you let the selection go, and until then the part that will go is drawn faint.

**Also**
• **⌘V** in the piano roll always pastes right on top of the copied notes, selected, even during playback; move the copy off with the arrows or the mouse
• Return tracks can be deleted in the arrangement, from the context menu or with Delete / Backspace; other tracks' sends stay on the right returns
• Multi-output instruments (Vienna Synchron Pianos on Windows and others) no longer crash Nota when added
• No more crash inside plug-ins after an export, a track freeze or applying audio settings
• Plug-ins work on Linux: VST3 plug-ins play sound again, and their windows draw and close properly
• Projects are now saved in format v21, which earlier Nota versions can't open
