Nota meets your hardware: plug in a controller and its knobs follow the device you're on, Nota Remote becomes a controller surface with an LED display, and the Session grows into a live instrument — played from the keyboard, turned on its side, and recorded straight into the Arrangement. Plus a new color-bass effect, Nota Color.

**1. Controllers that just work**
Plug in a controller and Nota recognises it: transport buttons drive the transport, faders move a bank of eight tracks, and the eight knobs follow the selected device, page by page, for every built-in device, rack and plug-in. Knobs pick up values instead of jumping, Mackie Control and HUI surfaces get motor faders, scribble strips and meters, and the chip next to MIDI in the top bar always tells you which track, device and page the knobs are on. Profiles ship for the Arturia KeyLab Essential 61 mk3, Novation Launchkey MK3 and Behringer X-Touch Mini; any other controller is taught in Set up…, and one function can be remapped later without redoing the rest.

**2. Nota Remote: Macros with an LED display**
On the phone, Macros now works like a controller: step through tracks, devices and pages, and a 128 × 48 LED display pictures the page — filter curves over the live spectrum, envelopes, LFOs, EQ and compressor curves, Reverb's real tail, keyboards for Chord and Scale. With Follow on, the phone and the hardware move together.

**3. The Session performance goes into the Arrangement**
Every Arrangement take now also records what you play in the Session: each clip lands on its track where it really played, whether you launched it by hand, by a scene, a follow action, a controller or the phone. One undo step, and Discard and retry take drops all of it.

**4. A Session you can play**
Return launches the selected slot, ⇧Return stops the track, ⌘Return launches the scene, and the slot buttons are almost twice as big. Turn the grid so scenes run across and tracks down. Cancel a queued launch before it starts, bypass one clip's follow actions, give an empty slot its own take length, and let a finished take launch the next scene. On the phone, the Session grid keeps its launch and Stop controls in view and shows groups.

**5. Nota Color**
A new audio effect for color bass: semitone shifts, tuned resonance and attack protection inside a band you choose, tuned to a scale, your own notes or another track's live MIDI, with the input and the shifted components drawn on the live spectrum.
