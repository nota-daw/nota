Nota 0.60.2 brings tempo changes into the arrangement as automation, an Assistant that answers from the manual, real device cards in Modular, and a long list of fixes from your reports.

**1. Tempo you draw**
Tempo is a lane on the master track now. Click in points, bend a segment for an accelerando or a ritardando, or put two points on one beat for an instant change. The grid, the metronome, warped audio, freeze, bounce and export all follow it, and the BPM field shows the tempo under the playhead. The master track also gets a pan knob, and every device on the master chain can be automated.

**2. An Assistant that knows the manual**
Ask a question in your own words, in English or Russian, and the Assistant panel (⌘⇧H / Ctrl+Shift+H) quotes the parts of the manual that answer it. Under a how-to, Show me opens the right view and outlines each control in turn. It works offline. The optional Manual search model also finds answers to questions worded differently from the manual.

**3. Device cards in Modular**
A Modular node shows the device's real, playable card, in XS, S or L. Drop a CV cable right on a knob to modulate it. Picking a parameter is now a searchable list, so even a plug-in with thousands of parameters opens at once.

**4. Grooves and pads that perform**
The Nota Rhythm bank (A–D) can be automated, so a lane can switch a groove to its fill on the downbeat. In Nota Remote, slide a held pad up or down to change Repeat's rate, or sideways to bend it on an MPE instrument.

**5. Fixes**
No more notes left hanging after edits, deletes or MIDI In changes. Drum Rack pads keep time while muted, choke groups no longer swallow hits, and input monitoring stops clicking when the interface runs at another sample rate. Several crashes in the piano roll are gone too.
