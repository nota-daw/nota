Nota 0.60.3 is a fix release: tempo-synced LFOs in Nota Aurora and Nota Volt now stay on the beat in the exported file.

<!-- changes -->
**Fixed**
• A pulse, gate or wobble from a tempo-synced LFO in Nota Aurora or Nota Volt lands on the same place in the beat on every play and in the export. It used to carry on from whatever played before, so a part that sat on the beat while you worked could sound off the grid in the exported file, or after playing from another bar ([c678a13](https://github.com/nota-daw/nota/commit/c678a13bf1c9f6019b63cf151dd9d5c59dafc680), [86030d2](https://github.com/nota-daw/nota/commit/86030d2dcf5a2514811c357ff46f7c542a9bcf17))
