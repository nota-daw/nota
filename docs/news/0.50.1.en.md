🎛 **Nota 0.50.1 is out**

A big one: the Session View is rebuilt from the ground up, your phone becomes a controller, and clips now move freely between the Arrangement and the Session.

**1. Session View, rebuilt for real work**
Clips have a name, a colour, a note or waveform preview and a length in bars; an inspector on the right edits the selected clip, scene or empty slot. Launch modes (Trigger, Gate, Toggle, Repeat), per-clip quantize, legato, one-shot and follow actions with chances. Scenes carry their own tempo and time signature and can follow on to the next one after a number of bars. Session Rec records straight into a scene — cut to whole bars or a fixed 1–8 bar length. Audio slots are full audio clips with the same editor as the Arrangement, warp included. Groups, returns, the master, a mixer under the grid and full keyboard control are all there.

**2. Nota Remote — your phone as a controller**
The **Remote** button shows a QR code: scan it and five seconds later your phone plays Nota. Pads, keys in the project's scale with chords, an XY pad with tilt, the mixer, macros and the Session grid. Nothing to install; it runs over Wi-Fi or a USB cable. Several phones can play at once, each on its own track, and Nota shows who plays what. Knobs and XY axes map through the same MIDI Learn as hardware.

**3. Clips between the Arrangement and Session**
⌘C / ⌘V works both ways, or press **⌘⇧C** to send the selection straight across. Notes, envelopes, warp, pitch and gain travel with the clip, and every copy is one undo step.

**4. Audio clips into instruments**
Drag an audio clip from the Arrangement onto a track with a Sampler, Nota Grain, Drum Rack or Instrument Rack and its audio loads there (a Drum Rack takes it on the next free pad). Drop a clip anywhere a sample goes.

**5. Nota Shutter · Pattern**
A new tab turns the gate into a tempo-synced rhythmic gate: draw steps on an 8/16/32-step grid, roll new patterns with the dice, add Smooth and Swing. The pattern can also drive the Modular view as a CV source. 15 new presets.

**6. Faster editing**
• **⌘⇧D** duplicates a time range across the whole song, **⌘⇧I** inserts silence.
• **Count-in** of 1, 2 or 4 bars before playback and recording (right-click the metronome).
• Double-click the number next to any slider to type an exact value: "−6", "1.5k", "250 ms", "1/16".

**Also**
• A cleaner main window: the project name leads the transport bar, group nesting shows in the colour stripes, a calmer Overview, and bar-style waveforms (the solid look is one setting away).
• AirPods and other Bluetooth headphones no longer go silent on macOS.
• Very short notes no longer get stuck.

Import audio no longer has a shortcut — find it in File ▸ Import audio; ⌘I inserts a scene in the Session.
