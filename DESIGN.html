01 · Foundation

Rules

Nota's design language: the work carries the colour, the interface steps back, and one accent marks what is live or chosen. DESIGN.md is the text version with every value, the tests that enforce it and the accepted exceptions.

One accent: live or chosenOrange is playing, selected, the playhead, the loop, a toggle that is on, menu hover, the power switch, and a value control while hovered or dragged. Nothing else.
Resting values are neutralKnob arcs, fader fills, curves and waveforms at rest are viz ink. A modulation arc keeps its source chroma.
Semantics by purposeMute amber, solo blue, arm and record red. Green is positive status and the meter's safe range.
Derived ink on solid fillsText on an accent, mute, solo, arm or track-colour fill takes the ink with the higher contrast.
Seam between, rule withinPanels and keys meet on the dark seam; items inside a panel are divided by the lighter rule.
Flat docked, blur only floatingDepth is a tone step, a 1px seam and a faint top bevel. Only floating layers cast a blurred shadow.
2 · 3 · 4 corners2 for inner keys, rows, clips; 3 for controls and cards; 4 for modals; 0 for docked panels.
Two faces, one splitGeist for words, Geist Mono for every number a user reads. Nothing under 7 px.
No shoutingSentence case, letter-spacing 0. Acronyms and units keep their case: LFO rate, Gain dB.
State changes colour onlyNever size, border width or position. Nothing moves on hover.
Real time is not animatedMeters, playhead and spectra jump to the value.
Opacity is not a stateMuted and disabled take the next ink step.
Numbers are set−3.3 dB · 72 % · 3.2 k · 120.00

Accepted exceptions

  • Device cards keep 700 × 260, so device text sits at 7.5–10.5 px.
  • Nine track roles; transport 44 with Nota's layout and order; no in-window menubar row; title bar 32.
  • Menu popups carry a seam ring without a blur.
  • Play turns green in Session; data hues go through Ink().
