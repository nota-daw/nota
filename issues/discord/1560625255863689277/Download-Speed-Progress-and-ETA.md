**Download Speed, Progress and ETA for Updates and Resources**

Display clear, consistent download information for Nota’s automatic updates and resource downloads, including plugins, sample packs and AI models.

**During each download, show:**
- Current download speed in Mbit/s.
- Estimated time remaining (ETA).
- Downloaded size and total size, such as 125 MB / 500 MB.
- Completion percentage and a progress bar.

**Keep the information useful and accurate:**
- Smooth speed and ETA readings to avoid distracting fluctuations, while responding to meaningful changes in transfer speed.
- Use readable units and durations, such as 45 seconds or 2 minutes 10 seconds. Distinguish Mbit/s from MB/s clearly.
- If the total size or remaining time cannot be determined, show an honest unknown or calculating state rather than a misleading percentage or ETA.
- Clearly indicate queued, paused, interrupted and failed downloads. Do not leave a frozen ETA appearing active.
- For simultaneous downloads, show per-download progress and, where useful, an overall summary.

**Distinguish download progress from installation:**
Downloading, verifying, extracting and installing should have separate, clearly labelled states. Reaching 100% downloaded must not imply that an update or resource is already installed and ready to use. Only show an ETA for a subsequent stage when it can be estimated reliably.

Present this information in a compact, readable layout that works consistently across themes, without interrupting the workflow or audio playback.
